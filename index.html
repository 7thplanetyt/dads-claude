How the cost of a message works
  • Input is what Claude reads: your message, any photos, and the whole conversation so far. Output is what Claude writes, including its behind-the-scenes thinking.
  • Long chats cost a bit more per message, because Claude re-reads the conversation each time. Repeated parts are cached at a big discount, but starting a New chat when you change topic keeps things cheap.
  • A photo counts as about 1,500 input tokens.
  • Answer style in Settings changes how long Claude thinks. "Thorough" gives better answers to hard questions and costs more.
  • The spend shown here is an estimate from this device. The Claude Console shows the exact amount.
